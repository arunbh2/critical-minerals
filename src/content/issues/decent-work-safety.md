## Why it matters

Workers carry many of the most direct risks of mineral development: falls, collapses, explosions, toxic dust, heat and chemical exposure. Contract and migrant workers often carry more risk with less protection. Informal workers in artisanal mining, collection and recycling may have no formal protection at all.

## Where the risks concentrate

- **Mining:** ground failure, heavy equipment, dust, heat.
- **Processing:** furnaces, molten metal, acids and toxic gases. The December 2023 furnace accident at a smelter in Indonesia's Morowali Industrial Park, which killed around 20 workers, shows the stakes.
- **Recycling:** battery fires, toxic fumes from burning and acid leaching in unsafe informal settings.
- **Extreme heat:** a growing risk for outdoor and enclosed industrial work alike.

## What good practice requires

Safety management that covers everyone on site, whoever employs them. Workers' freedom to organise and to refuse unsafe work. Independent inspection. Public reporting of incidents. Fair wages and decent housing. For informal workers, inclusion in transitions rather than exclusion from them.
