## Why it matters

When harm occurs, through an accident, pollution, displacement or abuse, what matters to affected people is whether it stops, whether they are compensated and whether it is prevented from happening again. Accountability systems are only as good as the remedy they deliver.

## Reading the record carefully

This portal keeps these distinct:

- **An allegation:** a report of harm, from workers, communities, media or organisations.
- **A response:** what the company or authority says in reply.
- **An official finding:** the conclusion of an investigation, court or regulator.
- **An evaluated outcome:** independent evidence of what changed.

For example, after the December 2023 furnace accident at a Morowali smelter, early statements and later reporting differed on the death toll and on the cause. That is why dates and attribution matter.

## Routes to remedy

- Courts and national human rights or labour institutions.
- Company and site-level grievance mechanisms.
- Industry and multi-stakeholder standards with complaints processes.
- Buyer and investor engagement.
- Public trackers that record allegations and invite company responses.

## What good looks like

Remedy that is accessible, timely, independent, free from retaliation, and designed with the people it serves. Its results should be published.
