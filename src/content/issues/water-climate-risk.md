## Two directions of risk

**Climate affects mineral development.** Heat, floods, droughts, storms and water stress threaten facilities, supply routes, workers' health and the communities around them. Tailings dams and waste stores face greater stress from extreme rainfall.

**Mineral development affects resilience.** Mines and processing plants change land cover, water availability and quality, and ecosystems such as forests, wetlands and coastal waters. They can also change who lives where, and how people earn a living. Each of these changes alters local exposure and vulnerability to hazards.

## Keeping terms distinct

These terms overlap but are not interchangeable:

- **Mitigation:** reducing greenhouse gas emissions, for example in the power used for processing.
- **Adaptation:** adjusting to climate change, for example redesigning water systems for drought.
- **Disaster risk reduction:** reducing the likelihood and impact of disasters through warning, preparedness and safer siting.
- **Occupational safety:** protecting workers from workplace hazards, including heat.
- **Pollution control:** preventing contamination of air, water and soil.
- **Rights protection:** ensuring people can access water, information and remedy.

Relabelling everything as "climate resilience" hides who is responsible for what.

## Questions to investigate

These are prompts for a specific place. They are not claims about any named location:

- Who protects contracted workers during extreme heat?
- How does an industrial area coordinate flood warnings and evacuation with nearby villages?
- Does water-intensive processing affect local access to water in dry seasons?
- Who pays for shared preparedness, and through what mechanism?

## Support mechanisms

Some international finance targets this area. The World Bank launched a Climate-Smart Mining Facility in 2019 to help resource-rich developing countries reduce the climate and environmental footprint of mining. Its current status is not verified here. See [finance records](../../finance/).
