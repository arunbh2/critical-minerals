## What happened

Indonesia stopped exports of unprocessed nickel ore from 1 January 2020, bringing forward a planned ban. Processing investment expanded rapidly, concentrated in large industrial parks.

## What the evidence shows

- **Industrial capture:** Indonesia is the largest producer of mined nickel and, with China, accounts for most recent growth in refined supply of key energy minerals.
- **Worker safety:** a furnace accident at a Morowali smelter on 24 December 2023 killed around 20 workers, according to later reporting.
- **Distribution:** not established by the evidence reviewed here.

## Why it matters for others

Other producer countries are studying the model. The lesson available so far is double-edged: policy can relocate processing, and the institutions that protect workers and environments need to grow just as fast.
