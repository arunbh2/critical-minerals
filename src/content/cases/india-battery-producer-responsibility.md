## What happened

In August 2022 India notified Battery Waste Management Rules that make producers, including importers, responsible for collecting and recycling the batteries they put on the market, with registration through the Central Pollution Control Board.

## What the evidence shows

The intervention and its legal mechanism are documented. Its effects are not yet evidenced in the sources reviewed here. That is why this case is labelled as an untested hypothesis rather than a result.

## Why it matters for others

Many countries are adopting producer responsibility for batteries. Tracking India's experience, including what happens to informal workers, would provide useful evidence for others.
