## What happened

India set up District Mineral Foundations in mining districts, funded by required contributions from mining leaseholders, to benefit people and areas affected by mining. The scheme guiding their spending is known as the Pradhan Mantri Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY).

## What the evidence shows

- **Collection:** more than ₹1 lakh crore had been collected cumulatively by early 2025, more than half of it in Odisha, Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand.
- **Spending:** analyses of official figures reported that roughly half had not been spent.
- **Outcomes:** we have not found or assessed evaluations of household-level outcomes.

## Why it matters for others

Many countries are considering how to share mineral revenue locally. This case shows the value of a binding funding stream, and how much depends on who controls spending and whether affected people take part.
