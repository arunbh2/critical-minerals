## How to judge a role in this field

Job titles in this area vary widely, and the same title can mean very different things. Before applying, ask: what decisions can this role influence, and who is it accountable to? Does it have resources and independence, or is it mainly reporting? What travel and safety expectations come with it? The [careers guide](../../) has more on assessing the substance of a job.
